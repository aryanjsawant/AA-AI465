TF-IDF stands for term frequency-inverse document frequency, a weighting scheme used to turn text into numeric vectors. Term frequency counts how often a word appears in a document, while inverse document frequency down-weights words that appear in many documents across the corpus, since common words carry little distinguishing information. Multiplying the two together gives high weight to words that are frequent in one document but rare across the whole corpus, which is exactly the signal that identifies what a document is about. Cosine similarity between two TF-IDF vectors measures the angle between them, ignoring vector length, which makes it a natural way to compare documents of different sizes.
