[CRITICAL EVALUATION CASE: Model Hallucination / Systematic Blindspot]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Task ID      : Subset Task #43 (GSM8K Test Question #99)
Input Prompt :
"Mary is an avid gardener. Yesterday, she received 18 new potted plants from her
favorite plant nursery. She already has 2 potted plants on each of the 40 window
ledges of her large country home. Feeling generous, she has decided that she
will give 1 potted plant from each ledge to friends and family tomorrow.
How many potted plants will Mary remain with?"

[Window 1 / Round 1] (Sampling 5 independent reasoning paths, seed base 7009900)
  Sample 1: "Mary has 40 * 2 = 80 potted plants currently on her ledges. Tomorrow
             she will give away 1 plant from each of the 40 ledges, so she gives
             away 40 * 1 = 40 plants. Therefore, she will be left with
             80 - 40 = 40 plants.
             Final answer: 40" -> Extracted: 40 [MISSES THE 18 NEW PLANTS!]
  Sample 2: "She already has 2 plants on each of the 40 ledges, so 2 * 40 = 80.
             She gives away 1 plant per ledge, which is 40 plants given away.
             Mary will remain with 80 - 40 = 40 potted plants.
             Final answer: 40" -> Extracted: 40 [MISSES THE 18 NEW PLANTS!]
  Sample 3: "Mary has 40 ledges * 2 plants = 80 plants. She gives away 40 ledges * 1
             = 40 plants. 80 - 40 = 40 plants remaining.
             Final answer: 40" -> Extracted: 40 [MISSES THE 18 NEW PLANTS!]
  Sample 4: "Total plants on ledges = 40 * 2 = 80. Plants given away = 40 * 1 = 40.
             Remaining plants = 80 - 40 = 40 plants.
             Final answer: 40" -> Extracted: 40 [MISSES THE 18 NEW PLANTS!]
  Sample 5: "Mary starts with 40 * 2 = 80 potted plants. She gives away 40 plants
             to friends and family. She remains with 80 - 40 = 40 plants.
             Final answer: 40" -> Extracted: 40 [MISSES THE 18 NEW PLANTS!]

Window 1 Answers : ['40', '40', '40', '40', '40']
Window 1 Latency : 21.65s | Completion Tokens: 312
-> STOPPING CRITERION MET: All 5 samples in Window 1 agree unanimously on '40'!
-> Early stop triggered at sample 5 of 20. Windows 2, 3, 4 skipped.

[Result & Diagnostic Failure Analysis]
  ESC Prediction : 40
  Ground Truth   : 58   [WRONG / INCORRECT CLASSIFICATION]
  
  *** ERROR ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS ***
  - Correct Step-by-Step Logic:
      1. Existing plants on ledges : 40 ledges * 2 plants = 80 plants
      2. New plants received       : +18 plants
      3. Total before gifting      : 80 + 18 = 98 plants
      4. Plants given away tomorrow: 40 ledges * 1 plant = 40 plants
      5. Final plants remaining    : 98 - 40 = 58 plants.
  - Why the 1.5B Model Failed:
      All 5 sampled generations suffered from an identical "distractor neglect"
      blindspot: the opening sentence ("Yesterday, she received 18 new potted
      plants...") was completely skipped in the reasoning chain by Qwen2.5-1.5B.
  - Why Early Stopping Was Vulnerable:
      ESC assumes that unanimity implies correctness (high confidence = high
      accuracy). However, on smaller open models (1.5B), systematic cognitive biases
      and sentence-skipping errors occur identically across random seeds.
      ESC halted at w=5, solidifying a WRONG consensus and saving compute on an
      incorrect answer.
