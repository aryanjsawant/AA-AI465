The naive rule 'if not at the right wall, go Right' has no memory of
where it came from, so once it bounces off the right wall it is
trapped between the last two squares and never returns to the dirt
it left behind.  One bit of state - the heading - is enough to
sweep the whole corridor; a full belief over the corridor is what
lets the agent know when to stop.
